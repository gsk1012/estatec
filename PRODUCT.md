## Platform

## Stack

Next.js 15 (App Router) + React 19 + Tailwind CSS 3 + TypeScript, mirroring the BlueStar Development "Telesto Motors" project setup. Deployed on Vercel.

## Users

- **Verkopers**: homeowners in Amsterdam and surroundings who want to know what their home is worth and sell it with a makelaar who handles everything.
- **Kopers**: first-time buyers, expats and international clients who find the Amsterdam market hard to navigate (often in a language that is not their first) and want an aankoopmakelaar who searches, advises, bids and closes for them.
- **Huurders and verhuurders**: tenants searching in Amsterdam, Amstelveen, Diemen, Haarlem and Hoofddorp; owners who want a property let with no vacancy.
- **Beleggers**: private and international investors buying, financing, letting and managing residential property; investors interested in co-investing in development projects.

All four groups carry equal weight on the homepage (confirmed by user).

## Product Purpose

ESTATEC is a full-service makelaarskantoor in Amsterdam (De Savornin Lohmanstraat 539, 1067 PV) with a strong focus on vastgoedontwikkeling. Services: aankoop, verkoop, aanhuur, verhuur, beheer, beleggen, hypotheken (vastgoedfinanciering), taxaties, and in-house vastgoedontwikkeling. Active in Noord-Holland, Flevoland and Utrecht. Success: a visitor quickly finds their route (sell, buy, rent, invest), trusts the office, and books a concrete next step.

## Positioning

A makelaar that is also an investor and developer: the office invests in, develops (renovatie, transformatie, woningsplitsing, kleinschalige nieuwbouw) and manages property itself, so advice on value, rental yield (NAR/BAR) and potential comes from first-hand practice. Founded by Akash Singh (residential investing since 2016). Personal, owner-led service that is reachable outside office hours and in the weekend; works with international clients and expats.

## Product Principles

1. Route first: every visitor (sell, buy, rent, invest) sees their path within one screen.
2. Proof over promise: real listings, real numbers from their own aanbod, real names and faces.
3. Owner-led, team-backed: Akash's reputation is the trust anchor, the rest of the team is visible.
4. Specific next steps: concrete CTAs (waardebepaling, kennismaking, zoekopdracht) instead of "neem contact op".
5. Technically clean: fast, zoomable, accessible, proper Dutch SEO.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Pinch-zoom must never be blocked. prefers-reduced-motion respected (hero video paused/poster). Meaningful alt text on all imagery.
